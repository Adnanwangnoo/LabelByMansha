Behind the label

Started at a cutting table, still run from one.

Label by Mansha began as a way to fix a problem: good embroidery was easy to find, good fit almost never was.

Our story

Clothing that fits the woman, not the size chart.

Mansha started stitching for family and neighbours before the label had a name — mostly wedding pieces that needed to survive a full day of ceremonies, not just a photograph. The requests kept the same shape: real tilla and dabka work, not printed imitations, and a fit that wasn't a guess.

That's still the whole business. We design a limited set of wedding wear, Kashmiri tilla and dabka work pieces each cycle, then cut every single order to the measurements you send us. Nothing sits on a rack waiting for the "average" body to walk in.

What grew is the range — bridal formals, reception wear, and a full Kashmiri tilla edit for the women who've been asking for years.

How a piece gets made

From bolt of fabric to your doorstep

Four stages, in the order they actually happen — nothing here is decorative.

01

Design & sourcing

Each cycle starts with fabric — pashmina, raw silk, georgette — sourced directly from mills, then paired with an original tilla or dabka embroidery pattern.

02

Your measurements

You send your size chart at checkout or over WhatsApp. No standard S/M/L — every panel is cut against your numbers.

03

Cutting & embroidery

Our tailors cut and stitch your piece individually. Tilla and dabka work is embroidered and checked by hand, thread by thread.

04

Press & dispatch

Every piece is steam-pressed, quality-checked against your order, then packed and dispatched with tracking.

What we won't compromise on

Four things every order is checked against

✦

Fabric you can verify

We name the mill and fabric type on every listing — no "premium fabric" without specifics.

✦

A real size chart

You measure, we cut. If a piece doesn't fit as promised, we alter it free of charge.

✦

Fair-paid embroidery

Our tilla and dabka artisans are paid per piece at rates we review every year, not the lowest bid we can find.

✦

Honest timelines

Made-to-measure takes 7–10 days, bridal pieces longer. We say so upfront instead of promising next-day and missing it.
