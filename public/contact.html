Before you ask

Frequently asked questions

How long does a made-to-measure order take?

Most suits are stitched and dispatched within 7–10 working days of us receiving your measurements. Bridal and heavily tilla/dabka embroidered pieces can take 2–3 weeks — we'll confirm a date before you pay.

What if the fit isn't right when it arrives?

Send us photos and the measurements you took — we'll alter the piece free of charge or, if it can't be fixed, offer a replacement or refund.

Do you ship outside India?

Yes, we ship internationally on request. Shipping cost and delivery time depend on your country — message us your location and we'll quote you directly.

Can I customise fabric, colour or embroidery on a listed design?

Most designs can be adjusted in colour or fabric weight for a small additional cost. Tell us the piece name and what you'd like changed.

What payment methods do you accept?

UPI, bank transfer and cash on delivery (select cities) for local orders; international orders are paid by card via a secure payment link.
